Delete your account

Required for App Store / Google Play account deletion

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the fourtillfour Check-In app and sign in.
  2. On Home, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. This removes your check-in app account and session data.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email support@fourtillfour.com with subject Delete Check-In account and include:

  • Full name on your loyalty account
  • Phone number used to sign in

We will delete your App account within a reasonable period after we verify the request. This does not automatically remove your GoHighLevel CRM contact used for cafe membership — ask staff if you also need that updated.
