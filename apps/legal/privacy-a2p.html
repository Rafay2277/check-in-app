What information is collected

What information is collected:

  • Name and phone number you provide
  • Mobile phone number and SMS opt-in / opt-out status
  • Membership and check-in related activity
  • Support messages you send us (email, phone, or text, including HELP/STOP)
  • Website cookie and tracking information as described below

How data is used

How data is used:

  • To identify members and provide membership services
  • To send SMS messages you consented to receive
  • To respond to HELP requests and process STOP opt-outs
  • To provide customer support
  • To operate and improve our website and services

SMS opt-in details

SMS opt-in details: If you opt in to SMS, fourtillfour may send recurring automated messages such as membership updates, reminders, check-in notices, customer care, offers, discounts, events, and service updates. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. To stop receiving messages, reply STOP to any text message. Reply HELP for assistance.

Clear 'No sharing' statement about SMS opt-ins (CRITICAL): No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Cookie & tracking info

Cookie & tracking info: Our website may use cookies, pixels, and similar tracking technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, understand traffic, and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features.

Data security practices

Data security practices: We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

User rights (unsubscribe, update info, etc.)

User rights (unsubscribe, update info, etc.):

  • Unsubscribe from SMS: reply STOP to any text message
  • Get help: reply HELP, or email support@fourtillfour.com, or call (602) 888-3583
  • Update your information: email support@fourtillfour.com with your name and phone number
  • Request deletion of your information: email support@fourtillfour.com

We may share information with service providers that help us run our website, membership program, hosting, and customer support. We do not sell personal information. We do not sell, rent, or buy leads.
